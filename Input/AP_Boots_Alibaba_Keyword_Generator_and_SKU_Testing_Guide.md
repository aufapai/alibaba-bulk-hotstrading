# AP Boots Alibaba.com Keyword Generator and SKU Testing Guide

**Company:** PT Indo Sukses Semesta (HOTS Trading)
**Product portfolio:** AP Boots
**Document status:** Basic working guide for Alibaba.com listing preparation
**Language:** English
**Confidentiality:** Internal working document. Do not expose internal floor prices, margin data, maximum discount ceilings, or internal exchange-rate rules to buyers.

---

## 1. Purpose

This guide provides a simple and repeatable method to:

1. Generate Alibaba.com product keywords.
2. Build clear product titles and buyer-focused listing angles.
3. Create differentiated listings without blindly duplicating the same product.
4. Present OEM/ODM capability with clear commercial conditions.
5. Test AP Boots SKU by SKU before expanding the workflow to the full 81-SKU master catalog.

The 81-SKU master catalog remains the product universe. The latest price list may contain fewer priced SKUs and must not be used to delete products from the master catalog.

---

## 2. Basic Keyword Principle

A good keyword should describe a real buyer search and a real product capability.

Use this basic structure:

```text
[Product Type] + [Main Material or Feature] + [Application or Industry] + [Buyer Intent]
```

### Example structures

```text
PVC Safety Boots for Mining and Construction Wholesale
Waterproof Work Boots for Agriculture and Plantation Use
Industrial Safety Boots with Steel Toe Cap for Distributors
Custom Logo Rubber Boots for Outdoor and Utility Brands
```

Do not place every possible keyword into one title. Each listing should have one main search intent and a clear target buyer.

## 7. Listing Differentiation and Duplicate Control

Do not create duplicate listings that have the same product, same title, same images, same application, and same buyer target.

A separate listing may be created when at least one meaningful commercial or search angle changes:

- Different product category or application.
- Different verified safety feature.
- Different size range or product format.
- Different material or outsole specification.
- Different buyer segment.
- Different packaging or private-label offer.
- Different OEM/ODM scope.
- Different country-market positioning.

### Recommended listing angles

| Listing angle | Main buyer | Example focus |
|---|---|---|
| Industrial safety | Safety manager, procurement team | Steel toe, midsole, verified standard |
| Agriculture and utility | Plantation, farm, fishery buyer | Waterproof body, mud tread, easy cleaning |
| Motorcycle and delivery | Fleet operator, courier supplier | Gear-shift guard, ankle protection, traction |
| Medical and food | Hospital, laboratory, kitchen buyer | Hygiene, slip resistance, material specification |
| Distributor supply | Importer, wholesaler | Box quantity, size range, wholesale supply |
| OEM/private label | Brand owner, importer | Logo, packaging, artwork, MOQ, sample approval |

A product can have several listing angles only when the content and buyer intent are genuinely different.

## 8. OEM and ODM Policy

HOTS Trading may support OEM/ODM inquiries, but OEM/ODM is **conditional**, not automatic for every SKU.

### 8.1 OEM definition

OEM means the buyer provides or approves the brand, logo, packaging, labeling, or other brand requirements while the product is made using an approved existing product platform or factory capability.

Possible OEM options may include:

- Custom logo or branding.
- Custom carton or retail packaging.
- Custom labels and barcode information.
- Buyer-approved color or material variation.
- Buyer-specific packaging artwork.

### 8.2 ODM definition

ODM means the buyer requests a product design or product modification based on an existing platform or a new development requirement.

Possible ODM options may include:

- Product shape or construction modification.
- New outsole or tread concept.
- New color combination.
- New size or fit requirement.
- Buyer-specific technical specification.
- New mold or tooling development.

ODM feasibility must be confirmed by the factory and technical team before it is promised to a buyer.

### 8.3 OEM/ODM qualification conditions

Every OEM/ODM request must be reviewed against these conditions:

1. **Product feasibility** - the selected SKU or product platform must support the requested modification.
2. **Factory confirmation** - WGI or the responsible factory must confirm production capability in writing.
3. **MOQ confirmation** - MOQ depends on product type, customization scope, color, packaging, tooling, and production method.
4. **Artwork and sample approval** - buyer artwork, logo placement, packaging, and samples must be approved before mass production.
5. **Tooling or mold cost** - any new tooling or mold cost must be quoted separately when applicable.
6. **Lead-time confirmation** - production lead time must be confirmed after specifications and sample approval.
7. **Payment terms** - deposit, balance, and any development payment must be confirmed before production.
8. **Quality control** - inspection requirements and acceptance criteria must be agreed before production.
9. **Compliance documents** - certification or testing documents must be verified for the customized SKU; existing certification must not automatically be transferred to a modified product.
10. **IP and confidentiality** - logo, artwork, mold, design, and buyer-owned specifications require written ownership and non-disclosure terms where applicable.
11. **Non-circumvention** - commercial engagement must follow the approved HOTS Trading agreement and communication process.
12. **Order cancellation** - customized or special production orders may have different cancellation conditions and must be stated in the quotation.

### 8.4 Safe buyer-facing wording

Use:

```text
OEM and ODM options are available for qualified projects, subject to product feasibility, factory confirmation, MOQ, artwork or sample approval, tooling requirements, lead time, and commercial terms.
```

For a specific SKU:

```text
Private labeling and custom packaging may be available for this SKU, subject to factory review, MOQ, artwork approval, and final quotation.
```

Avoid:

- Guaranteed low MOQ.
- Guaranteed certification after customization.
- Guaranteed tooling-free development.
- Guaranteed lead time before factory confirmation.
- Guaranteed exclusivity without a written agreement.
- Guaranteed compliance for a modified product based only on the original SKU.

## 9. Certification and Claim Control

Certification is SKU-specific.

- Only use certification claims explicitly confirmed in the latest approved source.
- Do not generalize EN ISO 20345 from one safety SKU to the whole safety group.
- AP Max must not carry a certification claim until written WGI confirmation is available.
- AP Chef and AP Medic are currently marked as having no certification in the latest internal price source.
- Non-safety, Terra, Orca, kids, football, and Vantopel lines must not receive certification claims without SKU-specific documentation.
- Do not add SNI, TKDN/P3DN, CE, HACCP, autoclave, or other compliance claims without supporting documents.

Recommended status labels:

```text
VERIFIED
CERTIFICATION_PENDING
NO_CERTIFICATION
CATALOG_ONLY
PRICE_PENDING_RECONCILIATION
MOQ_BY_TYPE
INTERNAL_ONLY
```

## 10. SKU Testing Workflow

Test a small representative set before processing all 81 SKUs.

### Phase 1: Data validation

For each test SKU, confirm:

- SKU exists in the 81-SKU master catalog.
- Product name and category are correct.
- Size and color are correct.
- Product features are supported by source data.
- Certification status is correctly labeled.
- Price is matched to the latest price source where available.
- PPN 12% treatment is correct for domestic calculations.
- MOQ is assigned by product type, not guessed.
- OEM/ODM status is `Yes`, `No`, or `Pending confirmation`.

### Phase 2: Keyword generation

Generate:

- 1 primary keyword.
- 8 to 15 supporting keywords.
- 3 title variations.
- 5 short selling points.
- 1 buyer application paragraph.
- 1 OEM/ODM paragraph only if qualified.
- 1 prohibited-claim list.

### Phase 3: Listing quality review

Check:

- The title describes the real product.
- The main keyword appears naturally.
- The application matches the product.
- The buyer intent is clear.
- The title does not overclaim certification.
- The listing does not copy another SKU's unique feature.
- OEM/ODM wording includes conditions.
- Price, MOQ, and packaging are not publicly exposed unless approved.

### Phase 4: Test publishing or draft review

Use a draft or controlled test listing first where possible. Review:

- Search relevance.
- Title clarity.
- Attribute completeness.
- Keyword duplication.
- Buyer questions.
- Inquiry quality.
- Incorrect claim risk.
- Whether the listing attracts the intended buyer segment.

Do not judge a listing only by impressions. Record inquiry relevance and buyer qualification as well.

## 14. Final Operating Rules

1. Use the 81-SKU master catalog as the product universe.
2. Use the latest approved price source only for matched current pricing.
3. Never delete a master SKU because it is missing from a price list.
4. Use PPN 12% for tax-inclusive IDR calculations.
5. Treat MOQ as product-type and commercial-term dependent.
6. Treat exchange rates as dynamic and date-specific.
7. Keep certification claims SKU-specific.
8. Treat OEM/ODM as conditional and subject to factory confirmation.
9. Do not promise MOQ, lead time, certification, exclusivity, or customization before approval.
10. Test representative SKUs before scaling keyword generation across the entire catalog.
